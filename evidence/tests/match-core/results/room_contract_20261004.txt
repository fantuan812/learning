What changed
- Only all-Accepted valid rooms with their own allocation may become Ready/Start.
- A pre-start Decline/Drop permanently invalidates that original member set. The chosen teaching policy waits for the original timeout and does not automatically requeue survivors.
- A room releases only the allocation it still owns. Cross Drop/Decline cannot requeue the same member repeatedly or consume another room's allocation.
- Existing room IDs, including Closed, are rejected before writes with invalid_argument. InProgress Drop is rejected; Settle owns that release.
These are serial, single-service memory contracts. They do not implement a production DS lease, concurrency or durable exactly-once processing.
